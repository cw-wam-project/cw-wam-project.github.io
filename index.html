02 / The idea

Plan motion.
Anticipate contact.

CW-WAM jointly models video, motion, and wrench. Its predicted wrench becomes a reference for feedback during execution.

01

Structure the interaction

Pair recent motion commands with measured wrenches. Pair-causal attention connects future actions and wrenches; long-term wrench memory retains earlier interaction information.

02

Predict paired references

Separate motion and wrench tokens, heads, and losses produce time-aligned chunks: a nominal motion plan and its expected contact wrench.

03

Keep feedback active

Use predicted wrench references for 20 Hz admittance corrections, with smooth trajectory tracking by a 1 kHz robot controller between model queries.

From prediction to feedback

Slow planning.
Responsive execution.

The controller compares measured and predicted wrench, corrects the nominal pose, and interpolates the resulting waypoints into a continuous trajectory.

03 / Evidence

Completion and contact quality.

Task outcomes show whether the robot finishes. Force measurements show how it interacts along the way.

↗

More successful insertions

CW-WAM improves peg insertion from 5/20 to 14/20 and adapter insertion from 8/20 to 19/20 compared with WAM. Insertion and subsequent release are reported separately.

≈

Closer force tracking

Adding admittance to CW-WAM reduces force-reference RMSE from 4.84 to 2.75 N for peg insertion and from 1.99 to 1.34 N for wiping, measured over successful trials.

↥

Motion that reflects load

Visually similar light and heavy loads stretch the spring scale differently. CW-WAM adjusts lifting motion to sensed load, improving lifting-height accuracy.
